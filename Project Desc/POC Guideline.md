**What should be in the threeminute video presentation?** 

**1. Problem Statement:** Clearly define the problem you want to address. Make sure it is aligned with any of the three hackathon tracks **.** 

**2. Proposed Solution:** Give an overview of your project and its essence. 

**3. Demonstration:** Run-through the project, live; show us how it works and how it actually solves the problem. 

**4. Pathway:** Direct us to your vision. How will we get to your MVP (minimum viable product), the very basic feature that can make your project feasible. 

**5. Wrap-Up:** Revisit your entire presentation with an impactful, 30-second closing.
